Logo design

Overview

Atalla Trading is a trading company. I designed their logo.

The logo

The logo is built on a bold letter A. A shipping container sits inside it, and a cargo plane flies out to the right, standing for goods moving by sea and by air. The name ATALLA TRADING runs along the bottom, following the angle of the container.

The palette is two colours: a deep navy and a light grey.

Colours

  • Navy #171E47
  • Grey #B2B2B2
