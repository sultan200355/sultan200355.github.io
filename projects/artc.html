Overview

The Arab Regional Training Center (ARTC) is a training center in Sharjah, UAE, with professional training programs and language courses.

ARTC was my first company, and the place where my design career began. I started there in 2018 in the call center. When they found out I do graphic design and marketing, they handed me their Instagram page. From there I took over their marketing, designed their posts, helped organise their events and designed their website.

The challenge

Everything was new: it was my first job, and a new way of thinking. The market in Sharjah is different from Dubai, so I had to understand how ARTC and their audience think. Sometimes they wanted posters full of text and design, and sometimes something very simple.

Handling the graphic design and the marketing at the same time was hard at the start. The website was easier, because the web company we built it with helped me understand how to work with ARTC.

Process

I offered to design their posters, and that grew step by step: first their Instagram page, then their marketing and media plan.

When ARTC started working with Julia, a web development company, we built the website together. I designed it in Figma, and we developed it together with Julia’s team. We improved it every year, and today it looks completely different from the first version. I still freelance for Julia today.

I also helped organise ARTC’s events, including the videos and photos.

Result

ARTC is still the best company I have worked with. We built a long relationship based on respect: they supported me when I was a beginner, and I always gave them my best advice. They respected my ideas, and I respected theirs.

I learned a lot with them, and they still contact me on WhatsApp when they need my help.
