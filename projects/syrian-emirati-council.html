Overview

The Syrian-Emirati Council for Entrepreneurs brings together Syrian and Emirati entrepreneurs, women and men, with a special focus on women in business. The council started two years ago.

Our team built their website and a complete online community with WordPress and plugins: a home page for the council, and a members’ community where they can talk about what they need and how they work. I am the council’s technical manager.

The challenge

The council has many members from many different fields, and they all needed to come together in one place. We had to build a community that suits all of them, in a style they feel at home with.

The members are experienced business leaders, and our team was young: I was the youngest engineer on the project. So our project manager led the conversations with the council, and we turned their wishes into the platform.

Process

We worked as a team of three: a project manager and two engineers. The manager gathered the council’s requests, and we built them together.

The other engineer focused on the design and the plugins. I worked alongside him as technical support: whenever an error came up, I was the emergency contact who fixed it straight away.

Gallery

Result

The council is happy with the result. Members started using the community, and it has received very good reviews.

What makes it special is that it works like LinkedIn, Facebook and WhatsApp in one place, so members can easily connect and talk to each other.
