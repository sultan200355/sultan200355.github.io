Beit Al Mukhtar

بيت المختار

“The Mukhtar’s House”: the home of the village chief

Grills & Butchery, Dubai

Client
Beit Al Mukhtar Grills & Butchery, Dubai
Year
2023
My role
Brand identity, logo, signage, packaging, print and vehicle design
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Canva (menu)

Overview

Beit Al Mukhtar is a grills and butchery restaurant in Dubai, opened by Lebanese and Syrian owners. They contacted me through the agency I worked with, and I worked on the brand from the first meeting to the finished restaurant.

The name means “the Mukhtar’s house”. In Lebanon and Syria, the mukhtar is the elected head of a village or neighbourhood: a respected local leader.

I met the owners, visited the location and talked through what they needed, their vision and the colours they liked.

The challenge

The owners wanted the brand to say “grills and butchery” at first sight. They loved the Arabic name, but the restaurant is in Dubai, so the main logo had to work in English while keeping the Arabic alongside it.

The packaging had a double job too: it had to serve customers who want quick fast food, and customers who order a full grill meal.

Process

I built the logo around a bull. The word BEIT sits between a pair of bull horns, with flames for the grill and two butcher’s cleavers at the sides, and AL MUKHTAR underneath.

I presented two colour concepts: one in dark brown, and one in light brown and orange. The owners chose the dark one. From there, the menu and all promotion followed a dark theme with the logo in white, while the letterhead uses light brown.

The identity then went everywhere the restaurant does: the sign on the building, the menu, flyers, the van wrap and a full packaging range, with boxes and bags for fast food and meal boxes for grills.

Result

The owners liked the identity and approved it quickly. The whole project took one month, from the first meetings and the site visit to the final print files.

They were happy working with me, and they recommended me to other clients.
