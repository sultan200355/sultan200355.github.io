Overview

I work as a freelancer for businesses in the UAE and the Netherlands: social media, print and websites.

I’m good with people. I listen, understand their ideas and make them real, at a fair price, so clients feel comfortable working with me. I also still work with Julia, the web company I met through ARTC, whenever they need me.

The challenge

Freelance work comes and goes: sometimes you are busy, sometimes it is quiet. Projects can also stop for reasons outside your control, as happened with BLU.

Process

I started studying digital marketing and graphic design in 2017. ARTC was my first company, and through them I met Julia, the web company I still work with today.

Now I work as a freelancer, so I can take on any kind of project. Next to that, I’m studying technical design to grow further into the technical side.
