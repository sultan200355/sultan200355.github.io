About

I’m Sultan Al Jaramani, a digital designer with Lebanese and Syrian roots. I lived in the UAE for 13 years, where I went to high school and started my career. Today I live in the Netherlands.

My studies in mechatronics gave me a technical base: Python, system analysis, PLC and SolidWorks. In 2017 I started studying digital marketing and graphic design, and since then I’ve designed brands, social media, print and websites for clients in the UAE and the Netherlands. Today I work as a freelancer.

I have a passion for art, technology and dogs, and I have a dog of my own. I speak Arabic and English fluently, and I’m learning Dutch: I understand it quite well, and I’m working on my speaking.
